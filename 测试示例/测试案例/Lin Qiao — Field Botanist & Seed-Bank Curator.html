About

For the past 12 years I've worked where climate, terrain, and time collide—documenting alpine plants and the communities that depend on them.

After a devastating wildfire season, I co-founded a community seed library to protect local biodiversity and restore what burns.

Today I advise cities and gardens on building climate-resilient plant communities and seed systems that last.
